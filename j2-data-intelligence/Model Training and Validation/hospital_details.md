Allocation Logic that apply:

🏥 1. Hospital Allocation Logic
In the Sri Lankan context, hospitals are not just about numbers; they are about tiers.

District/Divisional Hospitals: These are the primary hubs. Your data shows an average of 1.4 hospitals per ~80,000 people.

Rule: Allocate 1 Divisional Hospital for every 40,000–50,000 people. If a division is a "District Capital" (like Kandy city or Gampola), allocate 2 or more.

PMCUs (Primary Medical Care Units): These are for outpatient care.

Rule: Allocate 1 PMCU for every 15,000–20,000 people in rural or mountainous areas to ensure no one has to travel more than 5–10km for basic care.

🚑 2. Ambulance Allocation Logic
In Sri Lanka, ambulances are split between Hospital-based (for transfers) and Suwa Seriya 1990 (for emergencies).

Suwa Seriya (Emergency): The national target is 1 ambulance per 30,000 people or 1 per Police Division.

Hospital-based (Transfer):

Each District Hospital must have at least 2 ambulances.

Each Divisional Hospital must have at least 1 ambulance.

PMCUs generally do not have assigned ambulances; they call for Suwa Seriya.
